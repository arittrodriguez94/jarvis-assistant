Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail (read-only): subjects, senders and short previews of messages, to summarize email when the owner asks.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): upcoming events, to list the owner's schedule.
  • Google Drive (file names only): file names and links, to help find files. File contents are not read.

Jarvis cannot send, delete or change anything in Google.

How it is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computers. The AI model runs locally; Google data is not sent to any third-party AI service.
  • Results are shown only to the owner, in their private Telegram chat with the assistant.
  • Google data is not stored, sold, shared or used for advertising. The only thing kept is the sign-in token, stored on the owner's server.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
